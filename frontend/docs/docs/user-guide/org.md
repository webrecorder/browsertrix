# Introduction to Orgs

A Browsertrix org, or organization, is your workspace for web archiving. If you’re archiving collaboratively, an org workspace can be shared between team members.

## Subscription, Billing, and Quotas

`Paid Feature`{ .badge-green }

Each Browsertrix subscription includes one org, which is created for you when you register your account. You can customize the org’s [name and URL](org-settings.md#name-url-and-other-basic-information).

Billing is managed in [org settings](org-settings.md#billing). Depending on your plan, your org may have monthly quotas for storage and minutes of crawl time. These quotas can be increased by [upgrading your plan](org-settings.md#upgrade-downgrade-or-cancel-plan). Quotas refresh every calendar month.

Your subscription includes unlimited seats. However, if any member your organization would like their own, separate org, they will need to sign up for a new Browsertrix subscription.
